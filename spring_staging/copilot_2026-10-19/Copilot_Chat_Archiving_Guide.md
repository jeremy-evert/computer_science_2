Created from a Copilot conversation on exporting and preserving Copilot chat history.

# Key Takeaways

Microsoft provides export options for Copilot activity history through the privacy dashboard. Exports are typically downloaded as CSV files.

For long-term academic and teaching use, maintain your own archive of important conversations as Markdown, Word documents, PDFs, or Git-tracked artifacts.

# Recommended Workflow

1\. Export Copilot activity history periodically.  
2. Store exports in OneDrive.  
3. Convert important conversations into durable artifacts.  
4. Organize materials by course, project, or review cycle.  
5. Preserve decision receipts and evidence artifacts.
