## Ownership boundaries

- Week 1 is shared `semester_kickoff_week` material.
- Week 2 is a thin CS2 course wrapper around `local_ai_lab_setup` and
  `windows_classroom`.
- Monday Moments use the shared AI Fluency I source in `../ai_fluency/ai_i/`.
- Wednesday/Friday Professional Minds content is owned by
  `../professional_minds/`.
- This repository contains CS2-specific sequencing, gate evidence, rubrics,
  reflections, and course documentation. No deployment is implied here.
