Week 16 -- Farkle + Machine Learning

What are we learning? A shared applied experience across all Fall 2026 courses: using CS2 design thinking to evaluate a strategy contract with real evidence, not on which strategy 'feels' better.

What am I building/changing? This is shared-strand participation/evidence, not a weekly mini-project and not a fourth Odyssey checkpoint. Identify a stable strategy contract, compare two provided strategy/configuration choices, preserve reproducible evidence, and read/generate one honest chart.

How does my world connect? This week steps outside your world on purpose -- it is the one shared experience across the whole course, not a Reasoning Odyssey gate.

What evidence do I keep? An optional one-sentence World Bible line naming what changed your design judgment and the evidence that changed it -- no additional Odyssey artifact is required.

What exactly do I turn in? The graded Canvas assignment for this gate is now live -- find it in this module, in Assignments, or in Grades, and submit there using the submission types and rubric described above.

What carries forward? Week 17 is your final reflection, drawing on the full repository and World Bible -- not a new technical build.
