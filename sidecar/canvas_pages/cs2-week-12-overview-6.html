Reasoning Odyssey Gate -- Week 11 -- Data Storytelling / Flex Clinic (S09 + S07)

What are we learning? Evidence-backed storytelling -- communicating a claim from data (or, if not yet data-rich, reinforcing and defending prior work).

What am I building/changing? Communicate a concise evidence-backed story from your project/world data. Include a visual only when it serves the story. Name a limitation, uncertainty, or alternative interpretation, and make or defend a design or operational decision. If your project is not yet data-rich, a meaningful reinforcement of prior design, data, GUI, or testing work is equally valid.

How does my world connect? Builds directly on Week 10 own data, or falls back to strengthening whatever you have already built.

What evidence do I keep? Keep one concise World Bible entry: what changed, evidence used, and any remaining debt.

Success criteria (25 points)

CriterionPointsMeets expectation
Evidence-backed story / justified reinforcement8A real claim, backed by the student own evidence, or a meaningful reinforcement.
Supporting evidence7Concrete evidence backs the claim or reinforcement.
Limitation/decision reasoning / World Bible6Names a limitation or uncertainty and defends a real decision.
AI accountability4When AI was used: proposal, diff, independent test, reasoning, accept/reject decision.

What exactly do I turn in? The graded Canvas assignment for this gate is now live -- find it in this module, in Assignments, or in Grades, and submit there using the submission types and rubric described above.

What carries forward? Week 12 begins the synthesis/stabilization pass over everything built so far -- nothing new is introduced, the existing system is reviewed and hardened.
