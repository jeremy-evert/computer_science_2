Reasoning Odyssey Synthesis Checkpoint -- Week 12 -- Stabilization and Peer-Review Preparation (S08)

What are we learning? No new technical concept this week -- stabilization, testing discipline, and honest debt-tracking.

What am I building/changing? Freeze a substantial slice for review: improve tests, deepen any logging you have already been using so it still tells an honest story, improve runnable instructions, documentation, and known-debt record. None of this is new -- it is the testing and logging habits from Weeks 3-8, applied more carefully. Feature growth should be modest and justified.

How does my world connect? This is your whole system to date -- everything from Weeks 3-11 is now the subject.

What evidence do I keep? Keep one concise World Bible entry: what changed, evidence used, and any remaining debt.
