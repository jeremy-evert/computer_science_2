Quick answers

  • What do I choose? A real question about your own selected-world/project data.
  • What do I have to show? One or more readable, labeled visualizations that answer it, plus runnable code/data or a reproducible trace. Plotting syntax alone is not the evidence.
  • What do I have to explain? Why your representation is defensible, and one misleading alternative or visual pitfall.
  • What do I keep? One concise World Bible entry: what changed, the evidence you used, and any remaining debt.
  • Where is my data from? The same project system you have been building since your world began (Week 3 overview).
