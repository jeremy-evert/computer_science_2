Reasoning Odyssey Gate -- Week 11 -- Honest Visualization from Project Data (S09/S06/S08)

What are we learning? Honest data visualization -- choosing a defensible representation and naming what it could mislead someone into believing.

What am I building/changing? State a real question about your own selected-world/project data. Produce one or more readable, labeled visualizations that answer it, explain why the representation is defensible, and identify one misleading alternative or visual pitfall. Retain runnable code/data or a reproducible trace -- plotting syntax alone is not the evidence.

How does my world connect? This uses your own accumulated project data -- the same system you have been building since Week 3.

What evidence do I keep? Keep one concise World Bible entry: what changed, evidence used, and any remaining debt.
