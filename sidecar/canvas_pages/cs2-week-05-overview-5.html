Reasoning Odyssey Gate — Week 5 — Collaborating Objects and Invariant (S01/S08)

What are we learning? How objects work together — composition (has-a), collaborating objects, and protecting an invariant with a test. Just as important: recognizing when a job needs a partner object rather than a bigger single class or a hierarchy.

What am I building/changing? Refactor a real flow in your world into two or more collaborating objects using composition. Pick one thing that must always be true across that collaboration — an invariant — and protect it with an automated test that fails when it is broken and passes when it holds. Continue the Week 4 unittest habit. An optional first logging line at the invariant boundary is welcome, not required.

How does my world connect? Reopen your Week 4 object. Ask what it cannot do meaningfully alone — what does it need to collaborate with, and why? Composition, not a hierarchy: a Vault has-a lock; it is not a kind of Guard.

What evidence do I keep? One concise World Bible entry: what changed, what evidence you used, and any remaining debt. Explain in plain language why composition fits better here than an unnecessary hierarchy.

Whimsy hook — The Two-Key Vault

A vault that no single object can open. There is a Vault and two Keyholders, and the rule the whole system exists to guarantee is: the vault never opens unless both keyholders turn at the same time. Neither object is a kind of the other — they collaborate. Build the collaboration, then write the test that proves one key alone can never open it. Break the invariant on purpose and watch the test catch you.
