# CS2 repository map

| Area | Source of truth | Role in CS2 |
|---|---|---|
| Course-owned technical spine | This repo: `lessons/`, `planning/`, `assignments/`, `rubrics/` | Object design, contracts, data abstractions, GUI, visualization, and workflow evidence |
| Week 1 | `../semester_kickoff_week/` | Shared success-foundations kickoff; do not duplicate its handouts |
| Week 2 | `../local_ai_lab_setup/` and `../windows_classroom/` | Shared local-AI readiness and execution material; CS2 keeps a thin wrapper |
| Monday Moment | `../ai_fluency/ai_i/` | AI Fluency I, shared across Fall 2026 host courses; AI Fluency II is future work |
| Wednesday/Friday strand | `../professional_minds/` | Shared readings, slides, assignments, and rubrics |
| Portfolio-level conventions | `../swosu_cs_curriculum/` | Candidate destination for patterns that become reusable across courses |

The course repository is source preparation only. Canvas/Savnac deployment and
any generated objects belong to a later, separately authorized workflow.

## Local navigation

- `planning/` explains why a week exists and what it contributes.
- `assignments/odyssey_gates/` states what students submit.
- `rubrics/odyssey_gates/` states what evidence is gradable.
- `docs/grading-model.md` is the human-readable category and points map.
- `presentations/` is reserved for course-owned slide source; shared-strand
  decks stay upstream.
