### Course Learning Activities/Assignments

Transcript: Please turn in an unofficial transcript. And when you do, look at it closely. Make a comment if there is anything in there you did not expect.

Advisor Name: Turn in the name of your advisor and when you will meet with your advisor next. Please make a comment if you have had a hard time reaching your advisor in the past.

Degree Sheet or Degree Check: Please turn in a document that lists all the classes needed for someone to graduate with the degree you are working on. Please make a comment if you have any questions about what is required for you to graduate.

Personal Course Plan from Now until Graduation: Turn in a typed spreadsheet listing every semester you have remaining in your degree. Under each semester heading, please list the courses you will take that semester.

Resume: Create or update a resume with information about your work experience, education, and accomplishments to date. Save it because you will be building on it throughout the semester. If you are a recent high school graduate, you can leave on some high school accomplishments. If you have been out of school for a while, it might not be appropriate. When in doubt, please go see Career Services or the Writing Center.

Dream Job Description: What is your dream job? Find a description or job listing of it. This can be anything, but I want you to have a goal you are working for that helps shape your path. Please turn in a job posting or a written paragraph that details what a dream position would be for you after you finish your current degree. This can look like a traditional job, graduate school, a business plan, or even a travel plan. But tell me what your future could look like if things go well.

Gap Analysis: In less than a page, talk about the skills you are missing that might keep you from reaching your goals. Discuss how this class might help you fill that gap. Please turn in your analysis detailing the biggest gaps between your resume and a preferred candidate for your dream job.

GitHub Username: Please enter your GitHub User Name and accept an invite to the SWOSU shared GitHub repositories. If you don’t have one, please set up a GitHub account. Please enter the email address affiliated with your GitHub account.

Linked In Profile: Build a Profile and join the SWOSU Computer Science group. The LinkedIn profile should be something that you plan on using for a job search. This will also be a tool for you to keep in touch after graduation. This will allow us to tell you about job opportunities, mentoring needs, and other activities within the department. Over time, this will also provide a broader network to help with searching for employment.

You may search for the group, or follow this link:

https://www.linkedin.com/groups/14115199/

LinkedIn Learning: Please select a LinkedIn Learning Course and complete it. You set up your linked in account and select a course that is in line with your gap analysis. You will complete the course. Once the course is completed, you can open MY LIBRARY and then select the three dots next to the name of the course. This allows you to download a certificate. From there you can select “Download PDF Only.” Upload this PDF.

Please do 3 problems from this week’s Chapter. You may substitute a problem of your own creation based on that week’s chapter. Suggested problems are listed in Canvas. For the Choose Your Own Adventure Game (CYOAG) week assignment, please use any tools necessary to build a game of your choice.
