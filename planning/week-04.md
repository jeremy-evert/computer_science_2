# Week 4 — Collaborating Objects, Composition, and Invariants (S01/S08)

**Monday holiday — Labor Day.** There is no Monday class. Use the four-line
decomposition in the lesson to name the existing object, collaborator,
invariant, and smallest proof before Wednesday.

## Weekly focus

Reopen the cohesive object from Week 3 and refactor one real flow so two
objects collaborate through composition. Protect one meaningful invariant
with a focused `unittest` test and a short Friday trace. Compare composition
with inheritance only as a design decision; do not invent a hierarchy.

Read the [Week 4 lesson and worked example]({{link:week04_lesson}}), then
complete the [Week 4 Odyssey gate]({{link:week04_odyssey_gate}}). The [Week 4
rubric]({{link:week04_odyssey_rubric}}) is the scoring contract.

## Student path

| Day | Center | Student evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Wednesday | Refactor the real flow | object map, collaborator boundary, invariant statement, smallest code diff |
| Friday | Verify and defend the design | independent test, before/after trace, composition decision card, World Bible update |

Keep the AI Fluency connection explicit: **Decompose the Task** means split
the refactor into claims, inspect any proposal and diff, read the changed
code, test independently, and accept or reject from evidence. AI use is
optional; the accountability record is required when AI helps.
