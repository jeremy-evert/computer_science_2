# Show Your Work (start of semester, optional)

Start thinking now about what you will want to show by the end of this course. You do not have to do anything yet.

Skim the one-page guide: https://jeremy-evert.github.io/swosu-computing/show-your-work/

Three ideas to carry with you all semester:

- **Explain it:** if a stranger cannot tell what your work is in ten seconds, they will leave.
- **Credit it:** keep a running list of everything you did not make, as you go.
- **Test it as a stranger:** open your work in a private window before anyone else does.

Keep the list of what you make. In the last weeks you will pick one thing and show it.
