Quick answers

  • What do I choose? A claim from your own project/world data. If the project is not yet data-rich, choose earlier design, data, GUI, or testing work worth reinforcing.
  • What do I have to show? A concise evidence-backed story (add a visual only when it serves the story), or a meaningful, justified reinforcement.
  • What do I have to explain? A limitation, uncertainty, or alternative interpretation, and a design or operational decision you make or defend.
  • What do I keep? One concise World Bible entry: what changed, the evidence you used, and any remaining debt.
  • Where is my data from? The same project system you have been building since your world began (Week 3 overview).
