# Paired-programming report (A3)

## Purpose

Practice explaining object design, tests, and trade-offs aloud while reading a
partner's code. This is the Wednesday paired-work artifact, graded separately
from the Friday artifacts.

## Do

1. Work with a partner on the current CS2 Odyssey problem. Take turns driving
   and navigating, switching roles at least once.
2. If you share a repository, make your own commits. Explain the contribution
   split honestly; a lopsided log is a prompt to rebalance, not a reason to
   disguise authorship.
3. Record the problem, your design approach, what changed, how you tested or
   traced it, and what you personally contributed.
4. Disclose outside help or AI use, identify whose idea it was, and explain
   how you verified the result.

## Submit

An individual report with:

- the week's Odyssey gate and approach;
- your specific code/design contribution and a commit or file pointer;
- one thing you learned from your partner or changed after discussion;
- test/trace evidence and any AI proposal, diff, and accept/reject decision.

This is a 5% weekly category. It is not the Friday feedback report and does
not replace the Odyssey gate evidence.
