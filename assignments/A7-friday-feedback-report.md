# Friday feedback report (A7)

> **RETIRED (Prompt 030, 2026-08-25 — Decision 029 optional-Commons pivot).** This is no longer required, graded CS2 coursework; it is preserved here as provenance only. Pair programming and Show & Tell remain in-class practices with no Canvas assignment, reflection, receipt, or online equivalent. See `docs/grading-model.md` and `sidecar/reports/030_optional_commons_pivot_cs2_safe_execution.md`.

## Purpose

Practice giving useful, specific feedback on a classmate's CS2 demonstration.
This measures the feedback the student gives, not the quality of the
student's own Odyssey project.

## Do and submit

1. Watch or read one classmate's demonstration during Friday Show and Tell.
2. Give feedback in discussion or writing that is specific to the evidence:
   object behavior, test coverage, model/view boundary, chart choice, data
   story, or workflow reasoning as appropriate.
3. Report what you noticed, the feedback you gave, why it mattered, and one
   kind of feedback you would want on your own work.

An anonymous description is fine when appropriate; do not invent a response
to a demonstration you did not see. This is its own 5% weekly category,
separate from A4's reflection on the student's own work and from the Friday
Professional Minds lesson reflection.
