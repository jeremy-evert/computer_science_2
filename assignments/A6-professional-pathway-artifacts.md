## Purpose

Build career-planning evidence over the semester and explain the reasoning
behind revisions. The Week 1 baseline handouts are owned by the shared
`semester_kickoff_week` repository; this file defines the CS2 Week 14/15
course-local submissions rather than duplicating those handouts.

## Week 1 baseline — shared, ungraded

Use the shared kickoff artifacts for the transcript/degree audit, degree plan,
resume, GitHub/LinkedIn baseline, and initial target role. Keep the baseline
available so later changes can be compared against it.

## Week 14 update — graded 5%

Refresh target-role research, skill-gap analysis, resume, profiles, and degree
plan as needed. Submit a changelog/diff explaining what changed since Week 1
and why: target-role reasoning, new evidence, revised wording, or a new CS2
project signal. The reasoning trail matters more than polish.

## Week 15 completion — graded 5%

Asynchronously submit the completed set: transcript/degree audit, degree
plan, resume, GitHub/LinkedIn, target-role research, skill-gap analysis, and a
brief reflection on the next steps and why they follow from the evidence.
Week 15 has no new technical Odyssey gate; this is portfolio completion.

## Support and integrity

Bring an existing artifact when it already satisfies a requirement. Explain
the change or equivalence rather than recreating it. Do not claim a skill,
project, or profile update that the submitted evidence cannot support.
