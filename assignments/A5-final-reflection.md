# A5 — Final Reflection and Closure

## Purpose

This reflection is the final in place of a traditional technical exam. The
Odyssey repository is evidence of the journey, not a Week-17 feature sprint.

## Do

Choose five prompts, with at least one from each category, and use concrete
evidence from your World Bible/repository where helpful.

**Growth and joy**
- What did you enjoy, and what can you build or reason about now that you could not before?
- What design, abstraction, or event-driven idea finally made sense?

**Bugs and judgment**
- Which design mistake or bug taught you something, and what evidence changed your mind?
- What would you preserve or redesign if starting again?

**Community and tools**
- How did you help another person, and how did people/tools/AI help you?
- How did you accept or reject a proposal from evidence rather than narrative?

**Identity and next steps**
- What are you proud of?
- What kind of programmer/problem-solver are you becoming, and what comes next?

Review your World Bible for enough context that another person can understand
the project’s evolution. Specific, honest reflection matters more than polish.
