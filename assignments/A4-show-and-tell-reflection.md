# Show-and-Tell reflection (A4)

> **RETIRED (Prompt 030, 2026-08-25 — Decision 029 optional-Commons pivot).** This is no longer required, graded CS2 coursework; it is preserved here as provenance only. Pair programming and Show & Tell remain in-class practices with no Canvas assignment, reflection, receipt, or online equivalent. See `docs/grading-model.md` and `sidecar/reports/030_optional_commons_pivot_cs2_safe_execution.md`.

## Purpose

Demonstrate one working CS2 program or honest attempt, then reflect on the
technical judgment behind it during Friday Show and Tell.

## Do

1. Demonstrate a meaningful slice from the current week: an object boundary,
   collaborating design, data abstraction, GUI event, visualization, story,
   or reviewable workflow artifact.
2. Answer the four questions below in your own words, grounded in this week's
   code or evidence.
3. Keep the separate Friday feedback report for feedback given to a classmate.

## Journal — 10 points

| Question | Points |
|---|---:|
| What did you learn about the week's technical idea? | 3 |
| What else did you learn from testing, explaining, or seeing another design? | 3 |
| What could have gone better, and what evidence supports that claim? | 2 |
| What made a design choice, discovery, or revision feel special? | 2 |

Specific references to a bug, trace, event flow, chart decision, or design
conversation pass. "It went fine" is not evidence; an honest failed attempt
with a concrete lesson is welcome.

This is a separate 5% weekly category: it is about the student's own work,
not the Professional Minds lesson tie-in and not feedback given to someone
else.
