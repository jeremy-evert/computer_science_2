# Odyssey Gate — Week 16: The Reckoning, capstone scale

**Concept:** searching, sorting, Big-O reasoning, and full-semester integration (Deitel, §§11.6–11.14). **This week's gate is Checkpoint 4 itself** — see `assignments/A2-coding-odyssey-project.md` and `planning/week-16.md`. **Instruments:** full Build + Decide/Compare #2 (capstone) + capstone Debrief — see `docs/curriculum/judgment_toolkit.md` §2/§3/§4.

## The checkpoint (do this first)

Submit a playable/runnable capstone slice that uses world data across the semester: it must search or order meaningful data, show informed Big-O reasoning, and integrate earlier CS2 work (exceptions/modules/files, inheritance, recursion, or visualization) where it genuinely belongs. Explain what changed since Checkpoint 3 and demonstrate it.

## Decide/Compare #2 — capstone scale

Pick one still-open design decision. Commit to it before comparing at least one real alternative. Your comparison must include the actual trade-off for this world now; it may concern algorithm/data access (for example, linear versus binary search and the cost of maintaining sorted data), scope, or architecture. State why the comparison confirmed or changed your choice.

## Capstone Debrief (mandatory)

What is the most consequential thing that broke, was cut, or changed across the semester? Why did it happen? With your current understanding of CS1 foundations plus CS2 chapters 10–16, what would you design differently?

## World Bible

Log the final choice and rationale. This feeds directly into the Week 17 final Judgment Log review.
