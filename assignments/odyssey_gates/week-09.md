# Odyssey Gate — Week 9: Coding Odyssey Checkpoint 2 (inheritance in motion)

**Concept:** inheritance and polymorphism (Deitel, §§10.7–10.9). **This week's gate is Coding Odyssey Checkpoint 2 itself** — see `assignments/A2-coding-odyssey-project.md` and `planning/week-09.md`. **Instruments:** full Build + Debrief — see `docs/curriculum/judgment_toolkit.md` §2/§4.

## The checkpoint (do this first)

Submit a working world slice in which an inheritance family does work the world actually needs. Demonstrate a mixed group responding polymorphically, explain why the hierarchy is an is-a design rather than copied classes, and include a short run that makes the differing outcomes visible.

## Suggested shape (optional scaffolding)

- **Frontier Settlement:** a settlement advances a mixed colonist roster.
- **Investigation Bureau:** a case processes detective and analyst actions.
- **Starship Log:** a ship runs duty cycles for multiple crew roles.
- **Small Business:** a business runs a mixed employee shift.

## Checkpoint Debrief (mandatory)

What shared behavior did inheritance remove or clarify? What design choice proved awkward or broke, why did it happen, and what would you change before adding another subtype?

## World Bible

Log the checkpoint, the hierarchy decision, and the Debrief evidence.
