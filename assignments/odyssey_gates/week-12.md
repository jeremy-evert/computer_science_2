# Odyssey Gate — Week 12: Let the world show its history

**Concept:** plotting and data exploration with a simple visualization (Deitel, §5.17 and Matplotlib visualization sections). **Arc:** 3 — Worlds That Think About Their State. **Instrument:** Quick Check (pass/fail) — see `docs/curriculum/judgment_toolkit.md` §1.

## The gate (do this first)

Make one labeled plot from accumulated world-state data — preferably data your World Bible/project has collected over multiple events, not a chart of invented values that never enter the world. State one observation the plot lets you make.

## Quick Check (pass/fail)

- [ ] Code produces a displayed or saved plot from a sequence of at least four world-data observations.
- [ ] The plot has a meaningful title and labeled axes (or an equivalent clear legend/categories).
- [ ] The submission names one specific observation grounded in the plotted data.

## Suggested textbook problem (optional scaffolding)

- **Frontier Settlement:** food stores or population across turns.
- **Investigation Bureau:** leads resolved or case status across days.
- **Starship Log:** energy, hull integrity, or alerts across log entries.
- **Small Business:** sales, inventory, or customers across operating days.

Do this version directly if it helps, then let it *be* your gate submission.

## Then: open continuation (light Build, holistic)

Choose a graph type that tells the story honestly; explain an unexpected spike or flat line rather than hiding it.

## World Bible

One line: what you plotted, what you noticed.
